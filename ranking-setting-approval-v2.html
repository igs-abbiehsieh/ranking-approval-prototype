sciencePrototype guide操作說明

  1. Four buttons at the bottom: Cancel · Save Draft · Submit for Review · Agree & Approve. Each one lands on the Activity Setting list.頁尾四顆按鈕：取消 · 儲存草稿 · 提交審查 · 同意簽核。送出後都會進到活動列表。
  2. Save Draft → row shows Unsigned, Modify and Delete stay live. Modify goes back to this form.儲存草稿 → 該列顯示 尚未簽核，Modify、Delete 維持亮燈；按 Modify 回到設定頁。
  3. Submit for Review → row shows Pending Review, Modify and Delete grey out.提交審查 → 該列顯示 等待審查，Modify、Delete 灰階。
  4. Agree & Approve → disclaimer → row goes straight to Approved, buttons grey out.同意簽核 → 免責聲明 → 該列直接是 已核准，按鈕灰階。
  5. Time Zone in both dialogs follows the GMT selector in the header — change it and reopen to see it move.彈窗的 Time Zone 跟著右上角 GMT 選單走，改了再開就會變。
  6. Games and prize rows are live — edits flow into the dialogs and into the list row.遊戲與獎項可編輯，會即時反映到彈窗與列表那一列。
